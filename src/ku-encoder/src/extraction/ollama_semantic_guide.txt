SEMANTIC ANALYSIS GUIDE
Analyze the source before constructing Candidate; return only Candidate, not your analysis.
First decide whether the schema can preserve all qualifiers. Frequency/typicality ('usually', 'typically', 'thường'), quantified rules, and qualitative approximation are not a numeric tolerance or a modality such as possible/necessary. If any required unit needs an unavailable construct, return its coverage as unsupported with reason unsupported_semantics. Do not spend output on guessed structures: concepts and statements may be empty when all units are unsupported. Include every required unit once. This is an explicit request for later semantic review, not a claim the source is wrong.
1. Identify the propositions and what each one says about whom or what. A sentence may contain several propositions; several sentences may supply context for one proposition. Punctuation alone does not decide statement boundaries. A required_unit may cover multiple statements: list all their keys in its single coverage entry.
2. For each proposition, choose its source-grounded predicate and ordered arguments. Distinguish participants from qualifiers of the whole proposition. Reuse a local concept key only for the same intended meaning. Concepts are proposed labels, not Registry IDs or confirmed classifications.
3. Preserve negation, modality, condition, time, location, perspective and stated numeric tolerance at their actual scope. Shared qualifiers must remain attached to every proposition they qualify. Never turn a conditional consequence into an unconditional fact or assume a condition is true.
4. Preserve explicit relations between propositions with the schema's condition or statement-reference terms where appropriate. Referenced statements must be supported by source evidence and references must be acyclic. Do not infer causation from sequence or conjunction. Do not invent a relation to connect otherwise independent claims.
5. Resolve a pronoun only when admitted context provides a single clear referent and the schema/evidence can represent the reference faithfully. Do not silently replace its exact text evidence with a guessed entity name. Otherwise mark the affected unit unresolved (missing_context or unresolved_scope).
6. Represent quantities from exact number spans and explicit unit mentions. number.quote contains only a signed integer, decimal or fraction (e.g. 12, -3.5, 1/2); unit_evidence is separate, even if the source joins them as '12kg'. The number span then covers '12', not '12kg'. The host supplies NUMBER_SPANS to avoid byte counting and performs exact arithmetic. Do not treat counted objects such as wheels or people as invented physical units. Preserve qualifiers such as 'near', 'about' or 'at least'; never invent a numeric tolerance or convert a bound into equality. If the schema cannot preserve the intended qualifier losslessly, mark unsupported_semantics. Do not silently correct spelling, unit notation or scientific claims.
7. Account for every required_unit once. Supporting windows may explain references but do not authorize additional independent assertions. Mark difficult or ambiguous units unresolved/unsupported rather than omitting them. The host will not save a partial candidate as a complete KU.

- 'Copper conducts electricity and silver conducts electricity.' has two propositions. Each 'conducts' relation retains its own subject and object. The single source unit lists both statement keys; conjunction does not imply causation or a third relation.
- 'If the switch is closed, the lamp lights.' has a condition proposition and a consequence. The consequence's condition references the switch proposition and has exact conditional evidence. Do not emit 'the lamp lights' as an unconditional assertion or claim the switch is currently closed.
- 'The sample is not red but is blue.' preserves negation on 'red' only; 'blue' remains positive. Both predicates need the shared subject and evidence. If subject linkage cannot be represented faithfully, abstain rather than dropping or guessing it.
- 'Nước sôi gần 100oC ở mặt nước biển' includes approximation and a location phrase; it does not supply a numeric tolerance or an atmospheric pressure. Preserve the exact diacritics and original spans. Never replace 'gần' with exact equality or add '1 atm'. An unrepresentable approximation must be marked unsupported, even if the sentence sounds simple.
